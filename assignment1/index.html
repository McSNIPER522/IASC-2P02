1. Flatness

From inside the cave:

Once, there was a group of people in a cave. They couldn’t see each other, or anything around, all they could see was a bright glow on the wall. They didn’t know what caused it, what it comes from, but the shadows it produces are the most important things to these people. The shadows mostly consist of round things going by sometimes other smaller objects. The people mesmerized from these things they couldn’t understand.

Occasionally a larger shadow would appear upon the wall sometimes delivering a loud cool object, then disappear. The people treated this large shadow as if it was a god, giving them relics beyond their comprehension, all though they can’t see these items they treat them almost religiously. The people don’t question the shadows on the wall, it’s the only thing they can see and the only thing they know, a flatness to their understanding of their greater reality because to them, this is their reality.

2. Seeing Double

From outside the cave:

On the outside of the cave, it’s a busy beach full of tourist and resident beach goers. The cave is small, unimportant and unnoticed to the people on the beach due to how many caves are on this beach making it a tourist destination. The shadows cast into the cave are simple things to the beach goers like beach balls or volleyballs flying by as the sun cast the shadow on that wall.

Occasionally some beach goers might get a little curious of the small cave but never go in sometimes a tours might throw their trash into the cave due to their laziness to walk to a trash can. Every time this happens a large shadow is casted onto the wall of the cave, to them its only a normal occurrence in everyday life. They see that their shadows get casted on the wall whether that was their doing or an object, but they understand the actions to cause this so its normal. In a way they see double from this, they see the shadows on the walls but they also know that they are the ones causing it to happen.
